Citizens

  • Access to state services from smartphone anytime

    No need to spend hours on offline paperwork management

  • Payment before delivery

    Efficient system, where orders can be paid in seconds, within the system of service processing

  • Paperwork is now digital

    Getting the right documents was never easier and faster

  • Secured universal identification

    Access to all services with one personal profile

Businesses

  • Order services anytime, from anywhere

    Request documents and services online, with no office hours and no trips to the counter.

  • Pay in seconds, in one flow

    Payment is part of the service. Use your own provider or SIMBIOLPAY.

  • Connects to your systems

    Open APIs link to ERP, SAP, payment and identity systems.

  • Records you can prove

    Critical events are notarised on blockchain, and any record can be, to prove it's unaltered.

Government

  • Launch new services in days

    Design forms, workflows and documents with no-code tools, without waiting months for IT.

  • Payment before delivery

    Orders are paid within the service, before delivery. Every transaction is reconciled automatically.

  • Less paperwork, more capacity

    Each online order avoids a postal mailing and saves staff time (est. 15 min per transaction).

  • Compliance built in

    GDPR, eIDAS 2.0 and the EU AI Act are in the architecture. Data stays in Switzerland or the EU.
